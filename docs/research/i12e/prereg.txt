P2 — pre-registration, written and committed BEFORE the first arm call.
=======================================================================

Model:  the session default, claude-opus-5[1m] (no --model reaches the CLI).
Budget: <= 140 live calls.  Spent before this point: 15
        (1 quota preflight + 14 scenario recordings).

THE ARMS  (21 summary scenarios in corpus/validation, 10 of them owner-check
negatives, scored by the shipped score_scenario)

  (a) no retrieve node                                   1 repeat   21 calls
  (b) MemoryRetriever, raw records only                  2 repeats  42 calls
  (c) MemoryRetriever + knowledge, knowledge_boost=0.0   0 repeats   0 calls
  (d) MemoryRetriever + knowledge, knowledge_boost=16.0  2 repeats  42 calls
                                                        ---------------------
                                                         total     105 calls

(c) COSTS NOTHING AND IS NOT SKIPPED. `dry_identity.py`, offline, hashes every
rendered prompt of every arm: (c)@0.0 and (b) produce ONE sha256 over all
twenty-one prompts, 28501c05e2af051f, on this three-entry store as they did on
ADR 0193's one-entry store. Arm (c) IS arm (b), byte for byte, so (b)'s two
live repeats are (c)'s two live repeats and reporting them as one arm would be
reporting the same experiment twice.

WHY 16.0 IS ARM (d)'s BOOST. Chosen offline, before any quota, from the WORST
case: `dry_identity.py` runs the whole sequential arm under a stub whose fixed
answer refreshes no lesson, so ADR 0116's staleness demotion walks all three
down monotonically. On a 1.0 grid, 16.0 is the smallest value that keeps ALL
THREE lessons inside `render_retrieved_context`'s five bullets on ALL
twenty-one scenarios under that worst case (8.0 — ADR 0193's value, tuned on a
one-entry store — reaches 7/21 for the word-cap lesson and 10/21 for the
joined one). It saturates: 16, 17 and 18 give the identical prompt hash.

THE DECISION RULE, evaluated by aggregate.py and not by hand
------------------------------------------------------------
Let spread(arm) = max(repeat means) - min(repeat means) for that arm.
Let BAR = max(spread(b), spread(d)).

  dimension 2 moves 14 -> 17  IFF
      a second knowledge entry formed from a DIFFERENT signature   AND
      (d) - (b) on the mean  >  BAR                                AND
      (d) - (b) on the ten negatives  >=  0

  dimension 2 moves 14 -> 15  IF a second entry formed and that band fails
      (the capability is real; the benefit is not shown).

  dimension 2 does not move  IF no second entry formed.

THE KNOB
--------
  knowledge_boost's shipped default moves from 0.0 to 16.0 ONLY IF
  (d) - (c) on the mean > BAR, and a test then pins the shipped value to the
  measured one. (c) is (b), so this is the same comparison as above and is
  decided by the same number. Otherwise the default stays 0.0 and the ADR says
  the knob is now measured on a store where lessons compete and still not
  earned.

WHAT WOULD FALSIFY THE WHOLE INCREMENT
--------------------------------------
  - (d) <= (b): the second lesson costs what the first one bought, and the
    finding is that lessons crowd each other out of the five bullets.
  - (d) - (b) positive but inside BAR: three measurements now agree that the
    layer's benefit is not separable from repeat noise at this sample size.

Both are reportable results and neither is a reason to add a repeat, change
the boost, or re-record a scenario after the fact.
