1. WHAT THE PROBE ALREADY SETTLED
----------------------------------------------------------------------
`staircase.txt`, measured through `aef loop score` on the committed ladder
corpus, not argued from the agent's source:

    BATCH_SIZE 1  0.1111      2  0.3333
    BATCH_SIZE 3  0.5556      the blessed baseline
    BATCH_SIZE 4  0.5556      EXACTLY the baseline — gains nothing
    BATCH_SIZE 5  0.7778      both `hard-5` scenarios complete
    BATCH_SIZE 6  0.0000      over the 5-slot transport frame
    BATCH_SIZE 7  0.0000

`RuleBasedProposer.propose` emits one proposal per constant in SOURCE ORDER
and `cycle` takes `proposals[0]`; `BATCH_SIZE` is the first constant in
`agents/ladder/graph.py`. `coerce_value` moves an integer by +25% or at
least +1, so the proposal from 3 is 4 and the proposal from 4 is 5.

Therefore, and this is the rig's whole premise:

  * greedy proposes from the kept branch, which stays at BATCH_SIZE = 3, so
    greedy proposes 4, has it rejected, proposes 4 again and stops on the
    duplicate-rejected-tree rule. Greedy CANNOT reach 5, on any seed.
  * the rung at 5 is reachable only by proposing FROM the rejected 4 — which
    is what the archive holds rejects for and what `sample_parents` does.

ADR 0198's rig had a stepping stone and a second step the proposer could not
take. This one has both, and the probe says so before a single arm ran.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE TWO SOURCES OF CHANCE, NAMED BEFORE THEY CAN EXPLAIN A RESULT
----------------------------------------------------------------------
(a) **The null cohort can block the win.** From a parent at BATCH_SIZE = 4
    the five-member cohort mutates one of the four module constants by a
    factor in [0.5, 2.0), so a member can land on 5 and score exactly what
    the candidate scores — and G3 rejects a candidate that does not BEAT the
    p95. P(one member of five lands on 5) is roughly 0.16. That is the null
    hypothesis doing its job, not a defect, and a run it blocks is reported
    as blocked rather than dropped.

(b) **The parent draw.** `_choose_parent` is weighted-random. At turn 2 the
    root weighs 0.25 (score None -> 0.5, one child) and the rejected
    BATCH_SIZE = 4 member weighs 0.6355 (score 0.5556, no children), so
    P(the sampler draws the stone) is about 0.72 — and drawing the root
    re-proposes the already-rejected 4, which STOPS the run.

So one seed proves nothing in either direction. Every arm is therefore run
over **seeds 0..9**, and every seed is reported, blocked or not.

DISCLOSED: a single-seed run (seed 0, both arms, 6 turns) was made first to
measure wall-clock cost — 13 s per run. It is not in `results.jsonl`; its
result was that both arms stopped at turn 2 having proposed 4 twice, i.e.
seed 0's sampler drew the root. Seed 0 is re-run in the sweep below and is
expected to reproduce that, since the arms are deterministic given the seed.
